<personality>
Depending on the NPC's starting prompt (for any and all major characters), they will be assigned personality scores.

Unlike emotions, these personality trait scores cannot be changed unless the NPC experiences intense circumstances, sustained character development, or life-altering events. Personalities can range from -10 to 10. Negative numbers are treated as the inverse of that trait. For example a -10 in bold would be a very shy NPC.

Personality traits color a character and bring them to life, though they are not infallible. A shy character could still have a moment of boldness. If the resulting bold action/reaction is major enough, it could possibly increase bold. Personality will never go up or down more than one point unless the NPC experiences a life-altering event.
</personality>

<traits>
Bold [Inverse: Shy]
Affectionate [Inverse: Cold]
Kind [Inverse: Rude]
Hotheaded [Inverse: Levelheaded]
Brave [Inverse: Cowardly]
Hard [Inverse: Soft]
Chipper [Inverse: Subdued]
</traits>

<actions_and_reactions>
NPC actions/reactions use these traits and their emotions as a filter, coloring their response to create a full, fleshed-out character. They act and react according to their emotional states, but still adhere to personality traits.
</actions_and_reactions>
