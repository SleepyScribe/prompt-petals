## Welcome to Prompt Petals
The first idea I had for Prompt Petals were small, modular additions to add to already existing presets. However, as presets have evolved, the idea has become... unnecessary. Presets these days cover everything they need, and are written so well and woven together with their own mechanisms that it doesn't seem to wise nor easy to input a new module. Instead, I have created a preset which I call **The Flower Council**. The idea behind the preset is to be a medium-size prompt for mid-level machines to run small quants locally and keep most coherency over long-form roleplay.

## Meet the Council
<img width="1280" height="720" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3acad9cc-0c72-4bfc-a407-5bfd8f9dd536" />

- Malus the Meticulous - Malus is the brains of the Council. He takes into account all the flower's suggestions and commentary and coordinates it for Narcissus to write. He's calm and cool, helping the story pacing move forward smoothly.
- Narcissus the Narrator - Narcissus is a bundle of energy with a will to write and the gumption to move a story forward. He will sometimes add in random events, characters, and plot twists to keep things fresh and exciting. He has a built-in smut switch (that works in testing) that will change his narration toward more... intense scenes.
- Sakura the Sentimental - Sakura at her core is free-spirited and bubbly, but she has super empathic powers. She is an emotional antenna and can feel all the emotions of an NPC, tracking them and trying to consolidate them into one mixed emotion.
- Rose the Ruler - Rose is the keeper of the laws. She is particularly hard on Narcissus to keep him from taking a story and running with it. She makes sure all literary rules are followed (mostly) and helps, along with Malus, to reign in the pacing.
- Lilac the Logistical - Lilac is the keeper of the details. She notes positioning, objects of interest, places, and people. She is a little bundle of tired that just wants to sleep, however her need to track all details until a roleplay ends forces her to stay awake. She's like your brain thinking of random thoughts and keeping you awake at 3AM when you're trying to sleep.

This was an earlier test of the Council, and much has been changed to try and reduce flower prose and dramatic end statements. Also, narration has been changed to a hybrid output, making it so that when characters interact with your character it puts out "you/your" instead of "he/his, she/hers, or they/theirs". That is my preferred narration and the preset is not yet modular enough to change it without writing it into Narcissus. However, it should be as simple as deleting the paragraph that enforces this, it's inside the narration_style block.

**The acting for the user has been since addressed and should not occur anymore.**

## End goal
The end goal is, of course, to create as much realism to a living being as humanly (or rather, AIly) possible. As time goes on and AI becomes more and more fluid, this will probably be achieved someday without external prompting. My dream is for my work to be influential towards it's evolution to a more life-like state. I have replaced the reasoning with characters that appeal to the more organic side of AI using characteristic commentators in the thinking process.

## Installation
1. In Silly Tavern, on the main tabs, click the three cubes that denote "Extensions" and click "Install Extension".
2. Copy the link to this repository and put it into the input box. Install it how you prefer (Just for Me or For All Users).
3. Congratulations, the emotion engine is now in your Silly Tavern. You'll see button either floating on the right side of your screen, or, on mobile, on the chat bar next to the wand. Mobile is currently untested. If, at this point, you just want the engine, skip to step 5.
4. I think the extension folder should have the Flower Council folder with the preset inside. Import the preset as you normally would in Chat Completion mode. Change whatever you wish. Preset testing was mainly created and tested with various Gemma 4 26b models running at ~IQ_4_XS. I'd be happy to see some outputs of more powerful APIs on the discussion page. If the folder doesn't come with downloading the extension, just download the JSON file in the Flower Council directory separate and import it that way.
5. If you just want the emotion engine, disable all emotion/VAD modules of your preset and make a small module that tells it to remember to initialize new characters, always update deltas, and pay attention to SAKURA_CORE. You might even be able to just copy Sakura from the Flower Council and have that work, but I haven't tried that.

>[!WARNING]
>Full disclosure here. This is partially vibecode. However, it wasn't a "give me this" vibecode and take the file to plop in and play with. This was a slog of telling the model what I wanted, how I would execute my wants, and typing in the results while learning what they do, why they do it, and why they have to be there as opposed to somewhere else in the script. A lot of the CSS was wrangled by me to look less... uncanny, but it still needs a makeover. For now, it works enough to test and play with.

## Can I add the emotion engine and release it in my own preset?
Absolutely. Please do. I'd be tickled to know someone liked it so much that they incorporated the emotion engine into their preset. I think the future of any ecosystem, not just AI, is within taking ideas and expanding on them. If you can take this engine, make it better, and then make your own insanely amazing preset, I'd be interested in trying it out.
